About me

I'm Harruna Abdul Kudus, working as DevKelly. I'm a Computer Science student at Kumasi Technical University, and a solo full-stack developer who takes SaaS products from idea to paying users.

Most of what I build solves a real problem for a real business first: ARIZONA GBC runs a print shop's jobs, payments and customer SMS, Kellylodge gets students into hostel rooms without the WhatsApp back-and-forth, CampusCart gives KsTU students a safe place to buy and sell, and PromptForge turns AI into something non-technical people can actually use.

I work end to end: database design, backend, frontend, and deployment, usually from a terminal in WSL2.
